Research computation and paid work

Evidence and computation supporting skilled human work and founder decisions.

Research and computation

AI can compare cited market offers, calculate quantities and sensitivities, draft design alternatives, organise source records and prepare JIT material. Outputs retain their evidence and assumptions for human review. The business direction and decisions remain with Luke.

The software calculator prices defined work by usage. A model name identifies a rate benchmark, not an appointed agent or an operating role.

Paid work

Cost preparation, travel where applicable, installation, paid learning, supervision, testing and handover. Design and specialist work need their own scope and provider rates. Skilled workers validate methods and acceptance; AI-generated hours remain estimates until checked against a quote or observed work.

JIT material should enable another paid crew to perform the task using the actual site and room version. Teaching, filming, editing and mentoring consume paid time.
