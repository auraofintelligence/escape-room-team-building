Aura of Intelligence
Aura Genesis brings personal transformation, an emerging intelligent Aura, mineral craft and scientific exploration 
into one adventure. The participant chooses categories of life, develops a faithful personal record, reflects 
through past, present and possible best selves, and brings those reflections into action.
This document defines the experience, data architecture, research programme, manufacturing sequence and life 
surrounding sixty two-hour Genesis sessions. All 120 Genesis hours accumulate in HBOT at pressure. The wider 
immersion has its own activities and time. The companion Aura Teacher Training 60 Days develops super-people 
and civic space stewards of Earth and the solar system through increasingly demanding work on intelligence, 
relationships, science and shared systems.
The Geode develops in three versions: geopolymer first, crystalline second, and future crystalline with compute 
embedded into the shell third. Straddie quartz, an expanded material palette, etheric practice and non-structural 
interior Kintsugi gold seams give each assembly a traceable physical and personal identity.
The development direction reaches longevity escape velocity, increasingly general intelligence, superintelligence 
and stewardship beyond Earth. Each project advances through a clear question, a chosen experience or 
experiment, an observed result and a next decision. The participant's own words, permissions and changing 
understanding travel with that record.

5 Category led reflection and game progression
The participant selects a life area
The central game action begins with a meaningful category: relationships, health, home, work, resources, 
learning, creativity, beliefs, place, remembered travel or another area chosen by the person. Aura Matrix Studio 
supplies examples and an evolving interface for this minimum viable self. A category opens a broad conversation 
before deeper layers and subcategories are developed.
A cycle contains a declaration, exploration, a revised account and an intended action. The participant chooses a 
past self, present self or possible best self under particular conditions. The Aura reflects the account in the 
person's language and asks where it fits, where it misses and what context it needs. The person then chooses 
whether to continue, pause, revisit or try something outside the session.
Personal tools and polarity
Reflective tools include chakra perspectives, tarot, astrology, numerology, character maps, love-language 
exploration and personally created ideas. A participant describes the tradition, personal meaning and practice 
they use. Their own additions enter as named frameworks with their own history. The interface supports several 
interpretations and changing relevance.
Attraction and repulsion are recorded separately. A person might feel drawn to leadership and repelled by public 
scrutiny, or crave closeness while valuing solitude. Urges, cravings, remembered experience, desire to give and 
desire to receive all contribute to the account. A single positive-to-negative slider would lose these relationships.

The system begins with broad categories and expands subcategories when they help the person. Relationships 
might separate family, peers, partnership and community. Resources might separate time, money, tools,

Train through correction

linked to the exact summary and source. A model-training export includes permission, dataset version, the 
approved use and the corrections relevant to that use. Training and evaluation sets have separate purposes so 
that faithful repetition and useful transfer are assessed independently.

and retains the earlier statement. The model learns a conditional account of the person rather than treating each 
declaration as a timeless instruction.

7 Truth Engine baseline and interpretation
Build the baseline deliberately
Truth Engine participation begins with baseline identification. The team and participant select clearly described 
tasks that relate declared responses to synchronised sensor observations and session context. Baseline records 
include the prompt, the person's answer, the relevant time window, task conditions, signal quality and the 
analysis version. The fine-grained structure concentrates here before wider dialogue loops are expanded.
Repeated tasks explore ordinary variation: a familiar factual statement, a remembered event, an imagined 
situation, a deliberate role-play or a participant-labelled uncertainty. Labels identify what the person intended in 
that task. Context includes posture, speech, attention, fatigue and the pressure-session conditions relevant to the 
signal. A later response is compared with the recorded baseline using a stated method.
Probability and meaning share the record
Comparisons across time-series data produce probabilities, rankings and differences. Their display names the 
outcome being estimated, the data used and the conditions of the comparison. The participant's interpretation 
sits alongside that estimate. A response that appears inconsistent may carry a changed definition, a deeper 
motive, incomplete recall, discomfort or a new understanding of the question.

change, deeper meaning and chosen action have separate measures. Research on the engine compares its 
interpretations with independently reviewed tasks and participant corrections. The team tests generalisation 
across occasions and people using held-out data, preserving the limits of each comparison in the result record.

Flexible dimensions of experience

12 Geopolymer Geode
Straddie sand as a traceable starting material
Version 1 develops a cast mineral body with a quartz-rich Straddie sand base and selected additions. Each source 
lot receives photographs, chain of custody, assay and acquisition/use permissions. Work with Quandamooka 
custodians shapes locally sourced material and cultural knowledge entering the programme. Laboratory findings 
define the lot's actual mineral composition, particle distribution and impurities.
The recipe identifies aggregate and reactive binder ingredients separately. Candidate metakaolin geopolymer 
systems offer a starting experimental pathway. Recipe fields include dry mass fractions, particle-size bands in 
micrometres, activator composition, liquid-to-solid ratio, mixing order, cure temperature, humidity and time. 
Reinforcement and surface treatments have their own entries. The referenced geopolymer study links recipe 
variables with mechanical testing and microstructural analysis. [E1]
A disciplined casting campaign
A first campaign varies a small number of recipe factors while retaining a reference mix. Batch identifiers link 
mixing records, moulds, curing conditions and witness specimens. Repeated specimens estimate manufacturing 
spread. The team measures density in kg/m³, dimensional change in mm/m, water uptake by mass, open porosity, 
compressive and flexural response in MPa, and the failure location. A curved-panel campaign adds thickness 
variation, handling, attachment and interface behaviour.
X-ray diffraction identifies the mineral phases; composition assays and microscopy examine the relationship 
between feedstock, binder and crystal additions. Surface trials compare tactile finish, cleaning response, particle 
release and emissions. Environmental exposure tests use the temperature, moisture, cleaning and operating 
conditions relevant to the installed component. Acceptance ranges enter the test plan before a candidate is 
selected for that duty.
Designing the inhabited surface
The experience mock-up combines mineral panels, seat, lighting, display, sound and fixed capture hardware. 
Panel addresses map colour, texture, embedded specimens and any patterned acoustic cavities. Measurements 
include frequency response across positions, sound decay, panel vibration, luminance and glare. Participant 
review records perceived spaciousness, comfort, attraction, repulsion and the meaning of the blend.
Kintsugi gold seams sit within non-load-bearing and non-pressure-bearing inlays, lining panels or artwork. Each 
seam drawing identifies its substrate, joining medium, thickness, encapsulation, finish and attachment. Cleaning 
trials and electrical continuity measurements accompany the repair record. A seam's personal story links to the 
material record through the participant's chosen permissions.
The Version 1 deliverable is a reproducible cast assembly with separate compute, a serviceable panel system and 
a complete material history. The final dossier includes retained samples, manufacture records, measured 
properties, repair instructions and a rehearsal of the participant experience. These establish the material and 
operational foundation for the crystalline version.

13 Crystalline Geode
Making mineral order a design variable
Version 2 develops a crystalline Geode with separate compute. Its recipes describe the mineral phases, grain size, 
orientation, interfaces and porosity of the finished assembly. Candidate routes include bonded mineral mosaics, 
crystal-rich ceramics, controlled glass-ceramic structures and grown crystal components. Each route produces its 
own process and phase record, allowing the Geode's name and material description to follow the measured 
structure.
Traceable silica feedstock and selected additions enter small specimens before larger panels. A design study might 
compare oriented and unoriented mineral regions, two interface treatments, or different crystal fractions within a 
consistent panel geometry. Repeated fabrication provides an estimate of how closely the intended architecture is 
achieved. Witness samples preserve the state of each manufacturing batch.
Light sound and the material field
The material twin maps each panel, specimen cassette, seam and interface. Optical trials record transmission,
