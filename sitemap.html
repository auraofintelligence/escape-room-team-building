  1. Executive summaryDistinctive escape rooms across Australia, connected by journeys worth taking, skills worth carrying forward and fresh reasons to return.
  2. MarketCompare existing venues and customer segments, then examine which local opportunities could support this business concept.
  3. Network and tourismExplore how distinctive local rooms, linked travel and renewed experiences could support repeat visits across Australia.
  4. Design and deploymentCompare room formats, space requirements and delivery methods before choosing a premises or installation approach.
  5. Itemised economicsReview itemised supplier comparisons and explicit quantities, with the cost of unresolved scope left visible.
  6. FinanceUse worked calculations to inspect capacity, receipts, paid work and settlement, then identify what remains unpriced.
  7. Learning through lifeExplore how enjoyable independent play, reflection and later practice could support communication and transferable skills.
  8. Research and paid workExtend the founder's research and computational ability while keeping business decisions with the founder and skilled work with people.
  9. Renewal and rotationCompare renewal and rotation methods that could keep rooms useful, support repeat visits and make future costs visible.
  10. Partnerships and local valueExamine participation options for local businesses, councils and communities without presuming a commercial structure or partner commitment.
  11. Research and next decisionsUse the evidence to decide what is worth developing next, with timing derived from the work rather than an assumed opening date.
