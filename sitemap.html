Every chapter, the full source collection and downloadable editions.

  1. Executive summaryBuild a national adventure network that brings visitors to local businesses, turns shared challenges into skills for life, and gives Australians a fair go in the age of superintelligence.
  2. MarketEnter through a specific local opportunity, with a clear offer for each customer and a measured plan for every room.
  3. Network and tourismGive every destination its own character and every returning group a fresh reason to travel, contribute and learn.
  4. Design and deploymentBuild a complete operating room, then turn each installation into practical knowledge for the next paid crew.
  5. Itemised economicsEvery budget line has a quantity, a price or rate, a reason, a source and a status.
  6. FinanceFund the work, pay people properly, preserve renewal cash and make each participant's return understandable.
  7. Learning through lifeMake the experience useful beyond the room through specific noticing, richer language, repair and practice in a new setting.
  8. Proposed agent systemOne accountable founder, specific agent responsibilities and paid people carrying out clearly scoped work.
  9. Renewal and rotationKeep the experience fresh, the equipment dependable and the next renewal funded before it is due.
  10. Partnerships and local valueGrow a federation of useful local businesses, with shared learning and clear ownership of the work and its value.
  11. Programme and decisionsMove from an approved operating design to a complete paid pilot, then expand through costed local opportunities.
