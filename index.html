Executive summary

Distinctive escape rooms across Australia, connected by journeys worth taking, skills worth carrying forward and fresh reasons to return.

The business proposition

An Australia-wide escape-room network is the business concept under investigation: design distinctive rooms, connect local experiences into tourism journeys, and renew the catalogue so people have a reason to return. Enjoyment brings people through the door; reflection, just-in-time learning and practice in new settings could extend the value beyond the visit.

The intended connections with Aura of Intelligence and p4a-xyz-cinema bring personal learning, creative scenarios and civic participation into the research. Local stories and paid creative, technical and trade work are part of the opportunity. Joyful Responsible Abundance provides the wider direction: worthwhile experiences, responsibility to others and wider access to useful learning, work and opportunity.

What ROUND ONE establishes

This first round brings together market evidence, examples from existing venues, itemised cost comparisons and calculations for Luke Nathan Hayes to review. It tests how the business could work and what further evidence would make a decision worthwhile.

Existing operators demonstrate a market for leisure, school and corporate experiences, varied room counts and multi-game offers. They do not establish demand for this particular concept, its price or a future site. Local customer research, delivered costs and evidence of repeat use remain central to the commercial case.

Options and calculations for review

The chapters compare hosted rooms, a dedicated venue, installation and renewal services, and possible network distribution. They retain new and second-hand furnishing comparisons, supplier room packages, explicit labour hours and software usage calculations. A two-room format, a four-room 400 m² layout and the ticket and pass figures are worked illustrations, with their assumptions identified beside the calculations.

No premises, room count, price, calendar, technology, business structure, contractor arrangement or AI model is selected by those examples. Costs without a reliable scope and price stay unpriced. The financial tables show partial results under stated inputs, rather than a fully quoted startup requirement or profit forecast.

How to read the evidence

Source observations describe what a named operator, supplier or publication reports. Model assumptions describe quantities or relationships introduced to explore an option. Founder decisions record choices Luke actually makes. These are kept separate throughout the plan and its financial inputs.

The research date is 29 October 2026. Each source entry distinguishes a page rechecked for this revision from an inherited reference that still needs verification. Advertised prices carry their observation date; no requested supplier quotation is recorded. Worked illustrations are labelled at section level so their figures can be read together without repeating a caveat in every paragraph.

The immediate purpose is review of the opportunity, evidence gaps and alternatives. AI can extend research and computation; it does not determine the business or replace the founder's judgment.
