These are rate comparisons. Submitted audio minutes = recorded sessions × recorded people per session × minutes submitted per person × submission count. Full-length separate participant files each count; recording opt-outs reduce recorded headcount, while recorded teachers or facilitators add to it.

Token cost = calls × (input tokens × input rate + billable output tokens × output rate) ÷ 1,000,000. Add tools, storage, retries, hosting and paid human review. Standard short-context rates do not cover long-context, faster tiers or regional uplifts. The calculator keeps provider costs in USD until an AUD exchange rate and other invoice charges are supplied.

Scope still unpriced

Premises and outgoings; building and acoustic design; approvals; partitions and finishes; ventilation; electrical/data materials; original room design and rights; landed kits; multi-room capture and monitoring; software implementation and storage; insurance and professional work; management and founder pay; full roster costs; consumables; utilities; distribution; freight and make-good; financing and specific risks.

Each package needs quantities, tax basis, inclusions, exclusions and payment terms. The quote register retains these blanks. A supplier catalogue total cannot substitute for startup cash.
