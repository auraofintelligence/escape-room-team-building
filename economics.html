ROUND ONE · 05 · Itemised economics

Itemised setup and operating economics

Review itemised supplier comparisons and explicit quantities, with the cost of unresolved scope left visible.

The cost standard

The cost research separates advertised unit prices, assumed quantities and hours, and scope that needs a quotation. A displayed supplier price becomes a worked extension only when multiplied by a clearly stated quantity. It does not become a delivered project quote.

Price observation date: 29 October 2026, as recorded in the source register. Rechecked pages and inherited listings awaiting verification are identified separately. Supplier quotation date: none recorded. Freight, GST, condition, warranty, availability and installation scope remain part of each comparison.

The numerical baskets below are worked illustrations using the inherited quantities. Their subtotals help compare scope and expose omissions. They do not select equipment, furnishings, trades, software or a capital budget.

The delivery cost record

Each quoted work package identifies the drawing or room version, quantities, materials, labour roles, time, tools, delivery, installation, tests, training, warranty, payment milestones and exclusions. A supplier's game package and a complete Australian premises are different scopes.

A new-versus-used comparison needs to establish fitness for the task and remaining useful life. Durable tables, chairs, cabinets, desks, storage and scenic objects are useful second-hand candidates. Electrical, structural, fire, access, load-bearing and recording-system components require the specific suitability checks and professional work described in the site brief.

The detailed priced schedules and calculated scenarios follow below.

Software and AI unit-cost appendix

Worked illustration | 29 October 2026 | Quantities and business inputs are assumptions for comparison.

Worked software comparisons, dated 29 October 2026. Official OpenAI price documentation was rechecked for this revision. The named models illustrate published unit costs; they are not selections, subscriptions or a required architecture. Usage quantities are inherited model assumptions, not measured workload.

Transcription counts every submitted participant channel

One official price comparison is OpenAI's file transcription model, gpt-transcribe, at an estimated US$0.0045 per audio minute. The published live transcription comparison is US$0.017 per minute. [R121][R122]

Chargeable minutes equal sold teams × actual average recorded participants × minutes submitted per participant. The worked product mix assumes 5.15 participants and 45 minutes of gameplay. Separate participant files each count for their full submitted duration. This does not assume that silence is free or that six channels are charged as one. The volume calculation assumes separately submitted full-length participant files. A provider-specific multichannel rule would need verification for the actual service and endpoint. [R124]

At 50%, the calculation is 4,544 × 5.15 × 45 = 1,053,072 submitted audio minutes. The file example is therefore US$4,738.82 per year. The 5.15 average currently matches paying participants; any recorded teachers, carers or facilitators increase recorded headcount even if they do not buy a ticket. Recording the debrief, retrying failed uploads or submitting duplicate files adds its actual duration. Do not budget promotional credits or a silence reduction before they exist.

Post-play processing is one comparison option. Check actual turnaround, upload limits, accents, languages and noisy-room performance during the capture pilot before selecting a provider. Silent processing during play can also be tested without providing any in-game response. Actual turnaround determines the useful workflow.

Price research and computation by the work being compared

A current standard token-price example is gpt-6.1-sol at US$2 per million uncached input tokens and US$10 per million output tokens. Web search costs US$0.01 per call plus retrieved content tokens; file search costs US$0.0025 per call plus relevant storage and model usage. [R121]

The following two-call reflection package is an illustrative workload that could be measured in a future test. Input budgets include transcript, instructions and rubric; the second pass also includes the first draft. Output is for a qualified human facilitator to review. No cache or batch discount is assumed.

Proposed workCalls per teamInput token budgetOutput token budgetUS$ per team
Draft one team reflection from its authorised transcript and rubric112,0002,0000.044

The two-call package totals US$0.092 per team, or US$418.05 for 4,544 teams. Actual long transcripts, more revisions, additional analysis or larger models change the cost. This example does not claim that every team fits the token budget or that analysis is complete without human judgment.

For any provider, calculate each research or computation task as:

Runs × [(uncached input tokens × input rate + cached reads × cached rate + billable cache writes × write rate + billable output tokens × output rate) ÷ 1,000,000 + tool actions × their individual charges], then add actual compute, storage, connector and licence costs.

Count every model call and retry in a multi-step run. Include billable reasoning output where charged. A proposed planning deliverable using 20,000 uncached input tokens, 4,000 output tokens and three web searches costs US$0.11 at these unit prices, provided retrieved search tokens fit within the stated input budget. Its monthly total is US$0.11 × the approved number of runs, plus any other specified service costs. Monthly volumes remain unset because the work and frequency have not been chosen.

A task-cost comparison would record deliverable, runs, calls per run, input and output tokens, tool actions, retry count, storage and compute units, human review hours, monthly cap and actual spend. The calculation concerns the cost of producing material for review. It does not define an operating agent team or assign business decisions to software.

Currency and remaining software costs

The retained provider rate comparison is in US dollars. Account billing currency and invoice terms would need verification. [R123] Convert the actual USD invoice at the actual AUD paid per USD, add payment and foreign-exchange charges and applicable taxes, and deduct only eligible input tax credits. Do not mix the USD appendix directly into the AUD tables.

Booking integration, secure hosting, storage, backup, retention, workflow implementation, testing, maintenance, connector seats and additional human review still need their own quantities and quotes. The facilitator's existing time allocation covers review only if the pilot shows it fits; otherwise add the measured time. The published venue headroom is still before software and these other costs, so it remains a capacity to pay costs rather than a profit claim.
