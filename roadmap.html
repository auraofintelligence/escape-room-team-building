ROUND ONE · 11 · Research priorities

Research priorities

Evidence needed to develop a room location or network offer.

Questions for the next round

QuestionEvidence to obtain
Which customers will book and return?Responses to actual offers, local booking availability, acquisition costs and repeat use.
Which format fits?Measured rooms and support space, access, recording opt-out design and peak staffing.
What does delivery cost?Comparable scopes, dated prices, delivered new/used options, paid hours and payment terms.
Does the learning method work?Independent play, recording quality, review time, participant accounts and transfer into a different task.
Does another location add value?Its own demand and costs, usable JIT records, distinctive content and redeemed cross-venue bookings.

An operating test would answer a defined subset of these questions. Its scope and duration follow the question and cost. No opening date or rollout schedule is established. The research register records the resulting choices.
