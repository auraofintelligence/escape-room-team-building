# Escape Room Team Building

ROUND ONE Australian escape room network business plan

Luke Nathan Hayes | 29 October 2026 | ROUND ONE research and comparison edition

## Executive summary

Distinctive escape rooms across Australia, connected by journeys worth taking, skills worth carrying forward and fresh reasons to return.

### The business proposition

An Australia-wide escape-room network is the business concept under investigation: design distinctive rooms, connect local experiences into tourism journeys, and renew the catalogue so people have a reason to return. Enjoyment brings people through the door; reflection, just-in-time learning and practice in new settings could extend the value beyond the visit.

The intended connections with [Aura of Intelligence](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-genesis-geode-atlas/) and [p4a-xyz-cinema](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/p4a-xyz-cinema) bring personal learning, creative scenarios and civic participation into the research. Local stories and paid creative, technical and trade work are part of the opportunity. Joyful Responsible Abundance provides the wider direction: worthwhile experiences, responsibility to others and wider access to useful learning, work and opportunity.

Sources: D05, D07, P01, P10, P14

### What ROUND ONE establishes

This first round brings together market evidence, examples from existing venues, itemised cost comparisons and calculations for Luke Nathan Hayes to review. It tests how the business could work and what further evidence would make a decision worthwhile.

Existing operators demonstrate a market for leisure, school and corporate experiences, varied room counts and multi-game offers. They do not establish demand for this particular concept, its price or a future site. Local customer research, delivered costs and evidence of repeat use remain central to the commercial case.

Sources: M07, M09, M10, M18, M19, M20

## A tourism network that rewards return visits

Explore how distinctive local rooms, linked travel and renewed experiences could support repeat visits across Australia.

### The network product

A connected visitor journey is one way to develop the national network aim. A room could remain a complete local experience while also giving visitors a reason to try another destination. Distinctive stories, task structures and local connections would be important to the value of the journey.

Formats to compare include a local day with linked activities, a regional weekend with rooms and accommodation, and a passport used across a longer journey. Employer retreats and school series could add different forms of reflection and application. These are product concepts to research with their intended buyers.

Direct referrals, separately booked linked services and a package sold by a contracting operator create different support, pricing and settlement requirements. Comparing them does not establish partner participation or choose a selling structure.

Sources: D07, M06, M09, M22

### A reusable physical and story system

Room concepts worth comparing include practical coordination, investigation, shared-place decisions and journeys through changing conditions. Parallel tasks followed by a combined decision are one possible design pattern, rather than a settled room catalogue. Different participants can contribute information, observation, making, organising or explanation.

Independent play without hints is a design direction retained from the project brief. A room would need its necessary clues and instructions inside the game. Once play starts there are no hints from staff or AI, including vocabulary or teamwork prompts. Staff handle safety, access and equipment faults directly. The hard stop and reflection remain part of the experience even when the group has unfinished work.

Use replaceable puzzle cassettes, accessible service panels, labelled wiring, robust connectors, reusable furniture and a consistent reset state. Scenic elements can vary by place without changing every technical component. Each scenario has age and access settings, a parts list, solution record, reset checklist, fault response, version and independent-play test record.

### Transcription counts every submitted participant channel

One official price comparison is OpenAI's file transcription model, gpt-transcribe, at an estimated US$0.0045 per audio minute. The published live transcription comparison is US$0.017 per minute. [R121][R122]
