Demand and distribution

Checked 30 Oct 2026

TRA reports 112.5 million domestic overnight trips and $108.1 billion spending, plus 288.4 million day trips and $52.0 billion spending, in the year ending June 2026. Business-event visitors spent $17.2 billion in Australia during 2025. ABS records 4,160,918 school students in 9,673 schools in 2025. These describe the wider buyer context, not escape-room demand.

A local comparison needs competitor booking availability, reachable school and employer groups, visitor seasonality, transport, acquisition cost, repeat use and contribution by staffed opening block. Separate direct bookings, partner referrals and tourism distribution so commissions remain visible.
