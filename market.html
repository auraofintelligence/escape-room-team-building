Market and venue evidence

Enter through a specific local opportunity, with a clear offer for each customer and a measured plan for every room.

Four customer markets

CustomerReason to buyBuying pattern and commercial response
Public groups, families and visitorsEnjoyment, novelty, an occasion together and a memorable local experienceWeekends, evenings and holidays; clear group limits, reliable booking and fresh rooms for return visits.
Schools and youth organisationsCurriculum-linked excursions, communication and collaboration, an accessible shared challengeTerm-time blocks; transport, supervision, class size and reflection capacity determine the timetable.
Employers and business-event organisersTeam development, a useful retreat activity and application to real workWeekday and conference bookings; a defined workshop, facilitator and agreed group outputs.
Tourism partners and local operatorsMore reasons to visit, stay, return and spend locallyBookable routes, referral terms, live availability, reliable settlement and regionally distinctive content.

Tourism Research Australia reports 112.5 million domestic overnight trips and $108.1 billion in spending, plus 288.4 million day trips and $52.0 billion in spending, for the year ending June 2026. These are travel occasions and spending across tourism. The escape-room opportunity is assessed locally within those flows.

ABS records 4,160,918 school students in 9,673 Australian schools in 2025. TRA reports $17.2 billion in business-event visitor spending during 2025. The school register and business-event calendar are starting points for identifying buyers, timing and transport requirements.

The proposed $59 public price includes a structured reflection on the actual experience. The $99 workshop adds application to a chosen team or life situation. The quality of the rooms and facilitation must make those inclusions worth buying.

The distinctive offer joins independent play without hints, contextual noticing of language and teamwork, progression across life, local content, cross-venue travel and continuing personal Aura records chosen by the participant. Existing team-building operators also offer communication and reflection, so the business will compete through delivery quality, integration and repeat value.

A site-level market case

Each candidate record contains the measured premises and permitted use; local competitor prices, capacities and availability; reachable schools and employers; visitor nights and seasonal patterns; accommodation and event partners; transport and parking; suitable local contractors; and customers at each stage of interest, quotation and paid booking.

The sales forecast is assembled by customer segment, month, room session and expected group size. A school class that needs six room sessions consumes six sessions. A traveller playing three destinations generates three admissions and remains one customer in the customer count.

Compare direct bookings, partner referrals and tourism distribution separately. Retain the net amount after each channel's commission and payment fees. Track enquiries, conversion, advance bookings, cancellations, repeat visits, room utilisation and contribution per staffed opening block. Those results guide the next deployment.
