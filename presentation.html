Executive summary

Build a national adventure network that brings visitors to local businesses, turns shared challenges into skills for life, and gives Australians a fair go in the age of superintelligence.

I am building an Australian escape-room network alongside Aura of Intelligence and p4a-xyz-cinema. The business designs and deploys distinctive rooms, helps local operators run them, connects those experiences into tourism routes, and renews the catalogue so groups have a reason to return. Visitors buy an enjoyable shared adventure. Schools and employers can buy a deeper programme, and participating venues gain a route to repeat bookings, new customers and continuing practical support.

I lead the business with a proposed suite of AI agents. Paid labourers, tradespeople, designers, facilitators and specialist contractors carry out the work that needs their skills. The agents prepare research, specifications, schedules, comparisons, records and decision packs. The operating system described in this plan awaits my approval before any role is activated or given authority to contact people, hire, purchase or operate customer services.

Joyful Responsible Abundance gives the business its direction: experiences worth enjoying, responsibility for our effect on other people, and wider access to useful work, learning, ownership and opportunity.

03 / 11

A tourism network that rewards return visits

Give every destination its own character and every returning group a fresh reason to travel, contribute and learn.

A visitor can enjoy one room as a complete experience, then choose a linked journey through other participating venues. Each room has a different story, task structure and local connection. The network maintains room and scenario versions so returning groups can find genuinely unfamiliar experiences.

Three product lengths serve different customers: a local day with two experiences and an independent food or cultural stop; a regional weekend with several rooms and accommodation; and a longer passport used across a journey. Employer retreats add a workshop tied to actual work. School series can revisit the same capabilities through different room settings.

Partner businesses publish their actual offer, price, capacity, accessibility and booking arrangements. A package can be sold by a named contracting operator, or customers can buy linked services directly. The selling and settlement model is clear before bookings open.

05 / 11

Itemised setup and operating economics

Every budget line has a quantity, a price or rate, a reason, a source and a status.

The budget uses three clearly identified types of entry. A published unit price is multiplied by a stated design quantity. A work package uses explained task hours and a published wage or contractor charge-rate comparator. A site-specific item remains marked quotation required until its scope and price are available.

Published retail stock and sale prices are dated. Freight, GST, condition, warranty, installation and access to the actual stock are recorded separately where necessary. A second-hand chair is compared with a suitable new alternative through its delivered and usable cost, including any cleaning or repair.

Subtotals are labelled according to what they include. The model leaves unquoted work visible and calculates what the business could afford to pay for the remaining scope.

Finance, settlements and local ownership

Fund the work, pay people properly, preserve renewal cash and make each participant's return understandable.

AccountIncomeCosts and obligations
Local venueIts own admissions, workshops and agreed local servicesPeople, premises, delivery, maintenance, renewal, finance and applicable tax.
Deployment businessAgreed design, installation, commissioning, training and upgrade contractsMaterials, paid labour, contractors, freight, project work and warranty obligations.
Network serviceAgreed booking, support, scenario, distribution and renewal-service feesIts own people, systems, processing, customer service and contracted delivery.

A consolidated network report eliminates transfers between related entities. An independent partner's revenue stays with that partner. A room sale and the operator's later ticket sales are different transactions at different times.

Customer money committed to future services is tracked through the pass-settlement ledger. Grants, loans, equity, member capital and customer receipts each retain their actual financial character. An expression of interest and a guarantee are not cash receipts.

07 / 11

Language, teamwork and whole-life learning

Make the experience useful beyond the room through specific noticing, richer language, repair and practice in a new setting.

People first experience the challenge together. Microphones and task events preserve selected moments for reflection afterwards. The facilitator helps participants understand how their words, decisions and responses affected the experience, recognise something worth keeping, and practise something they want to change.

The learning begins in primary school and develops through adolescence, working life, family relationships and later life. Returning customers encounter new stories and tasks. The repeated capability might be listening, explaining, handling uncertainty, negotiating a boundary, sharing work or repairing an exchange.

The game runs without hints. Learning support happens in the reflection and any separately booked workshop. A later room creates another opportunity to use the skill independently.

08 / 11

Luke and the proposed agent suite

One accountable founder, specific agent responsibilities and paid people carrying out clearly scoped work.

I am the founder and accountable lead for this business. The proposed agent suite supports my decisions and, after approval, carries out the work allowed by its defined scope. Contractors and employees perform their agreed roles, with specialist work assigned to appropriately qualified people. Partner businesses retain authority over their own operations.

Every role has a brief, authorised sources, required outputs, handovers, account access, expenditure or communication limits, review conditions and a pause process. A role can receive standing authority within an approved scope. Exceptions return as concise decision packs.

The suite below is a system design for approval. It is not running. Publishing the plan does not create operating agents, business schedules, outreach, hiring or purchasing authority.

10 / 11

Local enterprise, councils and a fair go

Grow a federation of useful local businesses, with shared learning and clear ownership of the work and its value.

An existing escape operator can join distribution or install a new room. A tourism business can host a room or supply a linked experience. A local co-operative can own assets and employ people. A maker or trade business can deliver installation and renewal work. Agreements define the chosen relationship and its price.

The deployment business maintains the shared design, records and learning method. Local partners contribute their place knowledge, people, premises, stories and customers. The structure can support private businesses, co-operatives, community organisations and institutional hosts without requiring them to become one organisation.

Mutual Futures adds a route for succession and paid knowledge transfer from experienced owners. 500 Queens offers a connection for women-led enterprise, leadership and investment development. Participation and finance are developed through actual agreements.
